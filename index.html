IMPACT

How Are We
Recording Impact?

Total Clean Percentage (TCP)

The TCP (Total Clean Percentage) will be measured by pre-existing government and local cleaning efforts plus the area cleaned by our user base in proportion to the total area in the city.

Before and after pictures of areas will be taken during the events and reported to the app.

The app will display the total amount of trash picked up, along with local and regional leaderboards and the respective comparison photos.

EVENTS

Organize, Compete,
Make Impact Together

Join or create cleanup events

Events will be set up by the app runners, but other organizations can choose to set up their own event, or enter in as their own team. Users can receive alerts for local event pop ups or organized city clean ups.

The app allows for easy team creation, where friends can be invited to join events. Specific filters can be applied to categorize these events, for example forest, ocean, or park clean ups. In addition, they can be sorted by location, where the closest events will be displayed first.

Events can distribute different awards, such as cash based prizes, giftcards, or community service hours for their work.

Three ways to support

Our sponsors can help to support our events in 3 ways: funding, donations, or cash prizes. Sponsors can provide funds which organizers can use for the creation and management of their events.

They can also donate materials such as gloves, shovels, or food/drinks.

Rewards like gift cards can also be given for getting first on the leaderboards, or during rank level ups.
